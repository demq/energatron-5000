## 1. What this spec fixes

P5 brings the household's gas into the workspace: monthly gas bills (a landed PDF plus a YAML transcription of it),
a validated bill table, a gas usage profile, gas offers from CDR and `offer_manual`, a gas engine, a ranked gas cost and
a gas backtest. "Must" means required. D1 and the earlier specs stay binding; §14 lists every change this spec makes to
them.

**What P5 builds**
- core: `core/models/gas.py` (the `gas_bill_record` model and the gas table kinds), `core/gas_bill.py` (bill
  validation and the profile's periods, pure), `core/gas_tariff.py` (the `GasTariff` model), `core/cdr_gas_tariff.py`
  (CDR gas contract → `GasTariff` or exclusion reasons), `core/gas_engine.py` (the gas engine)
- adapters: parser `adapters/sources/gas_bill_record.py`; the CLI command `gas-bill new`
- services: `services/gas_bill.py` (the scaffold); stages `gas_bills`, `gas_profile`, `normalise_gas_offers`,
  `cost_gas`, `backtest_gas`
- `Params` v5, `RegionProfile` v4, `offer_fetch_log` v2, `offer_manual` v2 (§4, §5)
- no new third-party dependency

**Facts about the live CDR gas market** (probed 2026-11-05; public data)
- The plan list is the one P4 already requests (`fuelType=ALL`); gas list items carry `fuelType: GAS` and the gas
  distributor in `geography.distributors`. The metropolitan Adelaide gas network is named
  `AGN Metro/Barossa/Peterborough`; plans for other SA towns add `AGN Mount Gambier`, `AGN Port Pirie`,
  `AGN Riverland/Murray Bridge`, `AGN Whyalla`. Victorian plans name `Australian Gas Networks` (suffix `@VEC`), so a
  distributor match must be exact.
- Of the 28 configured bases, 9 list current residential gas plans for the metropolitan network: 73 plans in all.
- Structure seen in all 73 details (`gasContract`, `x-v: 3`):
  - `pricingModel` always `SINGLE_RATE`; `rateBlockUType` always `singleRate`; `dailySupplyChargeType` always `SINGLE`.
  - one `tariffPeriod` (`07-01`→`06-30` or `01-01`→`12-31`) in 69 plans; two (`06-01`→`09-30` and `10-01`→`05-31`, a
    winter and a summer price) in 4.
  - two or three step blocks in every plan: `period` `P1D` (daily allowances in MJ, e.g. 27, 49.3 or 109.589 MJ/day) in
    75 rate sets, `P1M` (a monthly allowance) in 2. `measureUnit` is never given; the unit is MJ.
  - `timeZone` `LOCAL` or absent; it doesn't matter for daily data.
  - discounts: guaranteed `percentOfBill` (23), conditional `PAY_ON_TIME`/`DIRECT_DEBIT` `percentOfBill`, conditional
    `OTHER` `fixedAmount`, one guaranteed `percentOfUse`. Fees: event fees only (`FIXED`, `PERCENT_OF_BILL`).
  - eligibility: `OTHER`, `THIRD_PARTY_ONLY`, `SENIOR_CARD`, `EXISTING_SOLAR`, `NEW_CUSTOMER`, `LOYALTY_MEMBER`,
    `ORG_MEMBER`; `incentives` in about half.

**Facts about the workspace** (structure only)
- One gas bill PDF waits in `data/landing/`; it lands as `gas_bill` (`--source gas_bill`, PDF creation date as
  `source_ts`). No gas data is landed yet; no legacy gas data exists.
- The bill is monthly. It lists one or more meter reads, each with a date, a read type (actual or estimate), start and
  end index (m³), heating value (MJ/m³), pressure factor and MJ; usage and supply lines (units × price = amount, prices
  GST-exclusive); a GST line and a total. The period's last read can be an estimate.
- The plan named on the bill is not among its retailer's current CDR plans, so the tariff in force can only come from an
  `offer_manual` (as P4.3.2 did for electricity).

## 3. Gas bills

### 3.1 `gas_bill` (unchanged)

The PDF lands as in D3 §5.6 (version 1, creation date). P5 adds **no text extraction**: the issue date and every figure
come from the record (§3.2), so no PDF library is needed (Q2). The plan's "issue date once P5 adds text extraction"
becomes "the record's issue date".

### 3.2 `gas_bill_record` v1 (`core/models/gas.py`, `adapters/sources/gas_bill_record.py`)

One YAML transcription per bill, written by hand or by an agent from the PDF:
```yaml
kind: gas_bill_record
schema_version: 1
pdf_dataset_id: gas_bill-<YYYYMMDDTHHMMSS>-<hash8>   # the landed PDF it transcribes
transcribed_by: owner | agent
retailer: <brand as printed>
plan_name: <plan as printed>
issue_date: "YYYY-MM-DD"
period_start: "YYYY-MM-DD"                            # inclusive
period_end: "YYYY-MM-DD"                              # inclusive
reads:                                                # in the bill's order
  - {date: "YYYY-MM-DD", type: actual | estimate, start: "<m³ index>", end: "<m³ index>",
     heating_value: "<MJ/m³>", pressure_factor: "<factor>", mj: "<MJ as printed>"}
lines:                                                # GST-exclusive, as printed
  - {line: usage | supply | discount | credit | fee | other, label: <text>, quantity: "<n>" | null,
     unit: MJ | day | null, rate: "<price>" | null, amount: "<signed $>", gst_free: false}
subtotal: "<$ before GST>"
gst: "<$>"
total: "<$ including GST>"
```
- **Conventions:** `rate` is dollars per unit, GST-exclusive (a bill printing c/MJ is divided by 100); `amount` is
  signed (credits and discounts negative). Only this period's charges are transcribed: `total` is their total
  including GST, never the amount due with a carried balance or payments. A line the bill applies after GST (a
  concession, a rebate) has `gst_free: true` (default `false`). Periods are inclusive: when a bill's end shares its read
  date with the next bill's start, the next bill's `period_start` is the day after.
- Every number is a quoted decimal string, read as `Decimal`; every date a quoted string (an unquoted YAML date is
  refused, `unquoted_date`, as for `offer_manual`). `extra="forbid"`.
- Model rules (fail construction, `E_INVALID_INPUT`): `period_start` ≤ `period_end`; at least one read and one line;
  `end` ≥ `start` per read (no rollover in P5); `heating_value`, `pressure_factor` > 0; a `usage` line has `quantity`,
  `unit: MJ` and `rate`; a `supply` line has `quantity`, `unit: day` and `rate`; other lines may leave them `null`.
- **No identifier fields.** The MIRN, account, invoice and reference numbers stay in the PDF; the record links to it only
  by `pdf_dataset_id`. The parser refuses a document with any key not in the model (so a pasted account number has no
  place to go).
- Parser `gas_bill_record` (version 1): detects YAML whose first lines hold `kind: gas_bill_record`; `inspect` loads with
  `yaml.safe_load`, refuses unquoted dates, validates the model; `source_ts` = `issue_date` at 00:00 UTC; `extra`:
  `pdf_dataset_id`, `period_start`, `period_end`, `source_ts_from: content`; `coverage` = the period; extension
  `.yaml`. Not a public source: the premises check runs and warns `W_PREMISES_UNCHECKED` (no identifier). `regions/
  sa.yaml` lists it.
- A corrected transcription is landed as a new dataset; the newest landed record per `pdf_dataset_id` is current (§6).

### 3.3 `energatron gas-bill new <pdf_dataset_id>` (`services/gas_bill.py`)

`new_gas_bill_record(ctx, pdf_dataset_id, *, force=False) -> GasBillScaffoldResult`. Read-only on the raw zone, no lock.
- The ID must be a landed `gas_bill` dataset (in `data/raw/gas_bill/_catalog.jsonl`), else `E_INVALID_INPUT`
  (`field: pdf_dataset_id`).
- Writes `data/landing/gas_bill_record-<pdf_dataset_id>.yaml`: the §3.2 skeleton with `pdf_dataset_id` filled, one
  empty read and the usual lines (`usage`, `supply`), `""` for every value, and comments naming each field. An
  existing file → `E_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED` unless `--force`.
- The skeleton doesn't validate (empty values); landing it unfilled fails with `invalid_gas_bill_record`.
- Result `gas_bill_scaffold_result` v1: `pdf_dataset_id`, `path` (workspace-relative). `--json` as usual.

## 4. Configuration

### 4.1 `params` v5 (`core/models/params.py`)

```yaml
offers:
  fuel_types: [ELECTRICITY, GAS]        # `Fuel` gains GAS
  gas_distributor: AGN Metro/Barossa/Peterborough   # region field: copied from the region profile when null
gas:
  reference_bills: 12                   # the reference year: the latest N bills (plan §10.8)
  full_year_days: 334                   # a reference shorter than this is a partial year (§9)
backtest:
  gas_tariffs: []                       # as `backtest.tariffs`, over bill-period dates
validation:
  gas_bills: {tolerance: "0.05"}        # $ per line and per bill (a decimal string); MJ checks: §6
```
- `offers.gas_distributor` joins `REGION_FIELDS`. `fetch-offers` needs each distributor only for the fuels listed in
  `fuel_types` (a `null` one for a listed fuel → `E_INVALID_INPUT`); a stage declaring `offers` is refused while either
  is `null`, as for P4's region fields.
- `backtest.gas_tariffs` has `backtest.tariffs`'s model and rules (snapshot IDs, inclusive dates, no overlap).
  `validation.backtest.tolerance_pct` serves both backtests.
- Migration v4 → v5: appends `GAS` to `offers.fuel_types` when absent, inserts `gas_distributor: null`, `gas`,
  `backtest.gas_tariffs: []` and `validation.gas_bills` with the defaults above.

### 4.2 `RegionProfile` v4 and `regions/sa.yaml`

Adds `gas_distributor: str` (`AGN Metro/Barossa/Peterborough` for SA) and lists the `gas_bill_record` parser. Migration
v3 → v4 inserts `null`; `build_context()` refuses a profile with it unset. One region, one gas network in P5; other SA
towns' networks wait for P8's region profiles.

### 4.3 `pipeline sync`

Fills `offers.gas_distributor` from the profile when `null` (`--check` reports `null` as drift), like the P4 fields.

### 4.4 `fetch-offers`: the distributor filter per fuel

The local filter's `distributor` rule (P4 spec §3.2 step 3) compares `geography.distributors` with
`offers.distributor` for `ELECTRICITY` items and `offers.gas_distributor` for `GAS` items, exactly (no substring). Every
other rule is unchanged. The fetch log becomes `offer_fetch_log` v2: `filter.distributor` stays (electricity) and
`filter.gas_distributor` (string or `null`) is added, and each `selected` entry gains `fuel_type` (from the list
item's `fuelType`); migration v1 → v2 inserts `null` and `ELECTRICITY` (a v1 batch selected electricity only). The `cdr_index` parser stays
version 1: its detection is unchanged and `inspect` validates through the model, which upgrades v1 logs.

## 5. Gas tariffs

### 5.1 The model (`core/gas_tariff.py`)

`GasTariff` (kind `gas_tariff` v1, frozen, `extra="forbid"`), GST-exclusive like `Tariff` (P4 spec §5.1):
```
offer_snapshot_id, plan_id, brand, retailer, display_name, source: cdr|manual, customer_type, distributors,
pricing_model, effective_from, effective_to,
seasons: [GasSeason{start: "MM-DD", end: "MM-DD", daily_supply, block_period: P1D|P1M,
          blocks: [GasBlock{volume_mj: Decimal|None, unit_price}]}],
discounts: [Discount], fees: [Fee],            # P4's models, unchanged
status: normalised|approximate, approximations, notes
```
- Partition rules: the seasons cover the 366 `MM-DD` values exactly once (P4's `seasons_partition_the_year`); every
  block but the last has a volume > 0, the last has none.
- The GST constants of P4 spec §5.1 apply unchanged to gas.

### 5.2 CDR gas mapping (`core/cdr_gas_tariff.py`)

`map_cdr_gas(data, plan, household, *, as_of) -> GasTariff | Excluded` is pure and reuses P4's `Plan`, `Household`,
`Excluded` and the value, eligibility, discount and fee helpers of `core/cdr_tariff.py` (shared through that module, not
copied). Every feature lands in exactly one of supported, approximation or exclusion:

| Feature | Treatment |
|---|---|
| `fuelType` not `GAS` | excluded `fuel:<type>` |
| `gasContract` missing or not an object | excluded `malformed:gasContract` |
| `pricingModel` other than `SINGLE_RATE` | excluded `pricing_model:<name>` |
| `rateBlockUType` other than `singleRate` | excluded `rate_type:<name>` |
| `dailySupplyChargeType` `BAND` | excluded `banded_supply` |
| seasons not partitioning the year | excluded `seasons_not_partition` |
| `measureUnit` absent or `MJ` | supported (MJ) |
| any other `measureUnit` | excluded `measure_unit:<unit>` |
| a block rate set with `period` `P1D` | supported: allowance `volume × days` (§9.2) |
| a block rate set with `period` `P1M` | supported: allowance prorated by days; approximation `block_month_prorated` |
| a block rate set with `period` `P1Y` or absent | excluded `block_period_unclear` (as P4) |
| a single rate, any period | supported |
| eligibility, discounts, fees, `incentives`, `greenPowerCharges`, effective dates, malformed values, `zero_priced` | exactly as P4 spec §5.2 |
| `timeZone` | ignored: gas data is daily |

### 5.3 `offer_manual` v2

Adds `gasContract` as the alternative to `electricityContract`: exactly one of the two is present (`E_INVALID_INPUT`
otherwise), in the CDR contract shape. `fuelType` of the mapped data is `GAS` for a `gasContract`. Migration v1 → v2
changes nothing but the version (every v1 document has an `electricityContract`). The parser stays version 1 for
detection; its `inspect` accepts v1 and v2. Snapshot IDs are unchanged (computed over the document as landed).

## 6. `gas_bills` (`services/stages/gas_bills.py`)

- Inputs: `records` = `data/raw/gas_bill_record` (+ catalog), `pdfs` = `data/raw/gas_bill` (its catalog only; blobs
  aren't read). Params: `validation.gas_bills` (`t`, dollars), `pricing.gst_rate`. Output `bills` = `data/interim/gas_bills`.
- Steps:
  1. Read the record catalog; `blobs_match_catalog` and `detector_known` as in P2/P4.
  2. Parse each record; one that no longer validates is a failed `records_valid`.
  3. **Current records:** per `pdf_dataset_id`, the newest by `landed_at`; the others are `superseded` (counted).
  4. Validate each current record (`core/gas_bill.py`, tolerance `t`):
     - `pdf_landed`: its `pdf_dataset_id` is in the `gas_bill` catalog.
     - `reads_recompute`: per read, `(end − start) × heating_value × pressure_factor`, rounded half up to the
       decimal places of the printed `mj`, is within one unit of that last place of `mj` (bills print MJ to 0, 1 or 2
       places).
     - `usage_matches_reads`: Σ `usage` line quantities equals Σ read MJ within one unit of the coarser printed
       place.
     - `days_match`: Σ `supply` line quantities equals the period's days (`period_end − period_start + 1`); a bill
       with a price change mid-period has two supply lines.
     - `lines_recompute`: every line with a quantity and rate has `|round(quantity × rate) − amount| ≤ t`.
     - `totals_recompute`: `|Σ lines without gst_free − subtotal| ≤ t`, `|round(subtotal × gst_rate) − gst| ≤ t` and
       `|subtotal + gst + Σ gst_free lines − total| ≤ t`.
     - `periods_disjoint`: no two current records' periods overlap.
  Every check fails the stage: a mismatch is a transcription error the owner fixes by landing a corrected record.
- Outputs:
  - `bills.parquet` (`gas_bill` v1): `record_dataset_id`, `pdf_dataset_id`, `issue_date`, `period_start`,
    `period_end`, `days`, `mj` (Σ read MJ, decimal128(18,6)), `end_read_type`, `estimated_reads`, `subtotal`, `gst`,
    `total` (decimal128(18,2)), `recomputed_total`; sorted by `period_start`.
  - `reads.parquet` (`gas_bill_read` v1): `record_dataset_id`, `position`, `read_date`, `read_type`, `start`, `end`,
    `heating_value`, `pressure_factor`, `mj`, `recomputed_mj`.
  - `lines.parquet` (`gas_bill_line` v1): `record_dataset_id`, `position`, `line`, `label`, `quantity`, `unit`, `rate`,
    `amount`.
  - `summary.json` (`gas_bills_summary` v1): counts (records, current, superseded), the first and last period dates,
    checks.
- `retailer` and `plan_name` stay out of the outputs (the plan name is public, but the pair locates a household's
  contract; the record keeps them).

## 7. `gas_profile` (`services/stages/gas_profile.py`)

- Input `bills` = `data/interim/gas_bills`. Params: `gas`. Output `profile` = `data/marts/gas_profile`.
- **Periods** (plan §10.6, `core/gas_bill.py`): bills in `period_start` order. A bill whose last read is an estimate is
  combined with the next bill when that bill starts the day after it ends; combining continues until a bill ends on an
  actual read, the next bill doesn't follow on, or no bill is left. A period's MJ is the sum of its bills' MJ (the next
  actual read corrects the estimate, so the sum is metered usage); `estimated` is true when its last read is an
  estimate.
- **Gaps:** a bill that doesn't start the day after the previous one ends leaves a gap; gaps are listed and warned
  (`W_GAS_GAP`), not failed (a missing bill is the owner's to fetch).
- **Reference:** the latest periods, newest first, until they hold at least `gas.reference_bills` bills (a combined
  period that straddles the count is included whole, so the reference may hold more bills); `reference_days` is the sum
  of their days (a gap inside the reference isn't counted). `partial_year` when `reference_days < gas.full_year_days`.
- Outputs:
  - `periods.parquet` (`gas_period` v1): `period_start`, `period_end`, `days`, `mj`, `mj_per_day` (decimal128(18,6)),
    `bills`, `estimated`, `in_reference`.
  - `monthly.parquet` (`gas_month` v1): `month` (`YYYY-MM`), `days_covered`, `mj` (each period's MJ spread evenly over
    its days: per month `mj × d / D` to 6 places, the period's last month taking the remainder), `mj_per_day`.
  - `summary.json` (`gas_profile_summary` v1): `periods`, `coverage_start`, `coverage_end`, `gaps` (date pairs),
    `estimated_periods`, `reference_start`, `reference_end`, `reference_days`, `reference_mj`, `partial_year`, checks.
- Checks (fail): `periods_contiguous_or_listed` (every adjacent pair either follows on or is a listed gap; an overlap
  fails), `mj_conserved` (Σ period MJ = Σ bill MJ exactly), `read_types_flagged` (every period's `estimated` is set
  from its last bill's last read).
- Weather normalisation (BoM heating degree days) stays a later option (plan §10.6).

## 9. The gas engine and `cost_gas`

### 9.1 Inputs to the engine

`price_gas(tariff, periods, *, scenario, pricing) -> Bill` (`core/gas_engine.py`, pure). A period is
`GasPeriod(first, last, mj: Decimal)`: inclusive dates and the MJ used. `Bill`, `LineItem`, `money` and the scenario
names are P4's (`core/engine.py`).

### 9.2 Rules

- **Seasons:** each date of the period belongs to the season of its `MM-DD`. Usage is spread evenly over the period's
  days (Q4): a season with `d` of the period's `D` days gets `mj × d / D`, quantised to 6 places; the last season in
  date order takes the remainder, so the season quantities add up to `mj` exactly.
- **Blocks:** within a season, block `i`'s allowance is `volume_i × d` (`P1D`) or `volume_i × d × 12 / 365` (`P1M`),
  quantised to 6 places; usage fills the blocks in order, the last takes the rest. One `usage` line per non-empty block
  (`label` `ALL`, `unit` `MJ`).
- **Supply:** one `supply` line per season with days in the period: `d × daily_supply`.
- **Discounts, GST, fees, one-off credits:** exactly P4 spec §7.6-§7.8, through the same code (the part of
  `core/engine.py` that settles a period's lines becomes a shared function; electricity results don't change).
- **Invariants (property-tested):** Σ usage quantities = `mj` per period; lines sum to the period total; no float;
  a one-season single-rate tariff gives `usage = round(mj × rate)`.

### 9.3 `cost_gas` (`services/stages/cost_gas.py`)

- Inputs: `offers` = `data/marts/offers_gas`, `profile` = `data/marts/gas_profile`. Params: `pricing`, `gas`. Output
  `costs` = `data/marts/costs/gas`.
- Billing periods: the profile's reference periods with their MJ. For each tariff × scenario (`conditions_met`,
  `conditions_not_met`): `price_gas`; `reference_cost` = Σ period totals; `annual_ongoing` = round(`reference_cost` ×
  365 / `reference_days`); `annual_year1` = `annual_ongoing` − one-off credits.
- Outputs: `line_items.parquet` (`gas_cost_line` v1, the columns of `elec_cost_line`), `totals.parquet`
  (`gas_cost_total` v1: `offer_snapshot_id`, `scenario`, `status`, `reference_cost`, `annual_ongoing`, `annual_year1`,
  `rank`, `approximations`; rank as P4), `summary.json` (`gas_cost_summary` v1: the reference dates and days,
  `partial_year`, periods, scenarios, tariff count, the three cheapest per scenario, checks).
- Checks (fail): `reference_present` (at least one reference period), `lines_sum`, `energy_conserved` (Σ usage
  quantities = Σ reference MJ, exactly). Warning `W_GAS_PARTIAL_YEAR` when `partial_year` (Q3).

## 15. Open questions / decisions for the owner

Each is decided here so P5 can be built; the recommendation is what the spec says.
1. **Separate gas stages** (`normalise_gas_offers`, `cost_gas`, `backtest_gas`) rather than widening P4's stages. The
   P4 outputs and kinds stay stable; P6's bundles read both marts.
2. **No PDF text extraction.** One bill a month is cheaper to transcribe than a parser to maintain (plan §10.6); a
   parser would need a new dependency. The PDF stays the evidence, the record its transcription.
3. **Annualise a short reference by days, and warn.** With one bill the annual figure is a rough scale-up that ignores
   winter; the warning and `partial_year` say so wherever it's shown. Ranking is still meaningful between offers.
4. **Usage is spread evenly over a bill's days** for seasons and daily block allowances. Bills give one MJ figure per
   period; GloBird's and AGL's terms prorate allowances by days in the same way.
5. **Eligibility types stay as P4 treats them:** a type is unmet only when listed in `offers.unmet_eligibility`. Gas
   plans add `SENIOR_CARD`, `THIRD_PARTY_ONLY`, `LOYALTY_MEMBER`, `ORG_MEMBER` and `NEW_CUSTOMER`; the owner can list
   the ones the household doesn't meet. P6's recommendation shows eligibility before anyone switches.
6. **`P1M` gas blocks are prorated by days** (`volume × d × 12 / 365`, approximation `block_month_prorated`), where the
   electricity engine applies them once per calendar-month period (P4 spec §7.2). Gas periods are bills, often not
   calendar months, and combined periods span several months, so a whole allowance per period would be wrong.
7. **The tariff in force is transcribed from the bill** (the plan isn't in CDR). The backtest then confirms the
   mechanics rather than the rates; a fact sheet for the plan, if the owner has one, would make it independent.
8. **The gas account number** isn't caught by the denylist as the bill prints it (the MIRN is: checked 2026-11-05 by
   counts only). The owner should add it to `accounts.gas` in `private/config.toml` and run `energatron denylist`; agents
   never edit `private/`. The record has no field for it (§3.2), so P5 doesn't depend on it.
